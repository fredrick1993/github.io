Focus Areas

Research Interests

My work sits at the convergence of microbial ecology, computational genomics, and biotechnology. Using 16S rRNA amplicon sequencing, whole-genome sequencing, and shotgun metagenomics, I reveal how microorganisms function within complex ecosystems.

From plant-soil microbiome dynamics and biocontrol to fermented food functional genomics, I integrate wet-lab experimentation with cutting-edge bioinformatics pipelines on HPC environments.

My PhD research at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville focuses on genomic and metagenomic approaches to understand microbial community structure and ecological roles across diverse environments.

Research Projects

01
16S rRNA Amplicon Sequencing & Metabolic Pathway Reconstruction
End-to-end pipeline from DNA extraction and library preparation through FASTQ QC, SPAdes assembly, and QUAST evaluation. Taxonomic identification via Prokka, NCBI, UniProt and KEGG. Reconstructed metabolic pathways to infer functional potential across microbial communities.

02
Tomato Microbiome & Biocontrol Analysis
Wet-lab investigation of tomato–microbe interactions. Isolated and characterised plant-associated bacteria, assessed biocontrol potential against phytopathogens and conducted greenhouse-based experiments measuring microbial effects on plant growth and disease suppression.

03
Microbiome Analysis Using QIIME 2
Sequence denoising, feature table construction, and taxonomic assignment. Alpha and beta diversity analyses comparing community structure across experimental groups. Full statistical reproducibility with publication-quality visualisations.

04
Metagenomics Analysis of Ugba Microbiome
Shotgun metagenomics and MAG recovery on HPC infrastructure to characterise microbial diversity and metabolic capacity of Ugba, a traditionally fermented African food. Functional genomics approach reveals unique ecological roles of the microbial community.

Bacterial Diversity in Urban Carbon Sequestration Trees — Salford, UK
Identified and characterised bacterial diversity in urban trees contributing to carbon sequestration in Salford City. Oral presentation at the University of Salford School of Science and Engineering, 2024.
